ABOUT US

Our Story

Sumba Tropical Luxury was born from a singular vision: to honor the untamed spirit of the forgotten island. We believe that true luxury lies in the preservation of the raw, the authentic, and the ancient.

A Philosophy of Intentional Living

Every element of our sanctuary is a dialogue between Sumba’s wild terrain and contemporary architectural elegance.

Authenticity

Direct connection to the local culture, preserving the sacred rituals and traditions of the Marapu people.

Sustainability

Operating with a circular mindset, from solar harvesting to zero-waste organic farming on our grounds.

Luxury

Refined simplicity. We define luxury as the abundance of space, silence, and time for contemplation.

Conscious Tourism

Earth & Community

We are guests on this island. Our commitment goes beyond carbon neutrality—we invest directly in Sumba’s future. Through our community fund, we support local schools, provide access to clean water, and promote medical initiatives for the remote villages surrounding our estates.

100% Solar Powered

Harvesting the intense Sumba sun to power our entire collection of villas.

Local Artisan Collaboration

Every piece of furniture is handcrafted by Sumbanese woodworkers using fallen timber.
