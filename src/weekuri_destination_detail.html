Weekuri

A hidden saltwater lagoon with crystal clear emerald waters separated from the ocean by majestic cliffs.

A Unique Saltwater Lagoon

Unlike ordinary lakes, Weekuri is a saltwater lagoon connected to the sea through a coral reef. So you can experience the sensation of swimming in a "lake that feels like the sea".

Destination Highlights

Legendary Sunset

Widely considered the most beautiful sunset viewpoint in Indonesia.

Calm Waters

Shallow, crystalline waters perfect for peaceful evening strolls.

Calm Waters

The iconic dwarf mangroves (Rhizophora Mucronata) found only here.

A Visual Journey

Locating Serenity

Weekuri is located approximately 25 km southwest of the town of Waitabula. It's easily accessible by a 30-minute drive.
